# REIE Boulder County Recorder Index Source Quality Evidence MVV

This certification records the source-specific Source Quality Evidence MVV for SRC-BOULDER-COUNTY-RECORDER-INDEX after exact Registry identity and County/Public Record conversion support were canonicalized.

The package is certification-only and limited to recorded-document index/search/verification metadata. It uses COUNTY_RECORDED_DOCUMENT_INDEX and RESTRICTED_OR_UNREVIEWED. It does not represent or authorize document images, scanned instruments, OCR, full text, document bodies, signatures, body-derived legal descriptions, certified-copy content, storage, redistribution, retrieval, scraping, customer display, or legal use.

The controlled evidence input is one exact-source CERTIFICATION_REFERENCE. Conversion delegates through the County specialization to the Public Record Core, then through Evidence Normalization, Source Quality Control, and Summary Assembly.

The result is COUNTY_EVIDENCE_CONVERSION_VALID over PUBLIC_RECORD_EVIDENCE_CONVERSION_VALID with a certification-only linkage. Normalization and Control remain INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; sparse Assembly accepts the supplied source. Eligibility is READY_WITH_KNOWN_GAPS.

Rights, technical access, freshness, attribution, and provenance remain UNKNOWN or incomplete. Fee status is unasserted. Registry authorization remains AWAITING_PROVIDER_CONFIRMATION, activation remains BLOCKED_NOT_AUTHORIZED, and claim eligibility remains false.

This package does not authorize Manifest inclusion and preserves PUBLIC_OR_GOVERNMENT_SOURCE_NOT_UNRESTRICTED_OR_VERIFIED_OR_COMPLETE, INDEX_OR_SEARCH_METADATA_NOT_DOCUMENT_CONTENT, and the separation between Registry, Source Quality evidence, activation, retrieval, display, and legal use.
