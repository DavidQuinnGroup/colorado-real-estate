# Boulder County Open Data Counsel Review Packet 1

This packet is for counsel review only. It is not legal advice, does not issue final legal conclusions, and does not authorize activation.

## Review Questions

| ID | Topic | Question | Provisional status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CR-01 | License hierarchy | When Boulder County catalog, ArcGIS item, and service metadata differ, which license and disclaimer governs REIE use? | Counsel review required |
| CR-02 | Storage and transformation | Can REIE store and transform selected public records into durable internal evidence after provider confirmation? | Counsel review required |
| CR-03 | Customer display | Can REIE publish derived non-predictive editorial summaries without exposing raw records, parcels, or account identifiers? | Counsel review required |
| CR-04 | Field exclusions | Which fields must be excluded from ingestion, persistence, customer display, search indexing, or generated copy? | Counsel review required |
| CR-05 | Hazard and safety datasets | Should emergency alert or hazard-adjacent datasets remain outside REIE absent a separate compliance program? | Not approved |

## Proposed Initial Conditions

- Use only provider-confirmed datasets.
- Persist only through a future authorized evidence schema.
- Exclude owner/person data and account-level public display.
- Use derived aggregate observations only.
- Include source attribution, access date, freshness, and limitations.
- No forecasting, safety claims, hazard advice, property-specific scores, or investment recommendations.
