# REIE Market Update Narrative And Intelligence Quality 2.0 Certification

Status: `PROJECT_ATLAS_MARKET_UPDATE_NARRATIVE_AND_INTELLIGENCE_QUALITY_2_0_CERTIFIED`

Human test state: `READY_FOR_EXECUTIVE_MARKET_UPDATE_NARRATIVE_QUALITY_2_0_HUMAN_TEST`

## Purpose

Market Update Preparation 2.0 turns already-admitted, dated market evidence
into a more useful Agent briefing without changing the evidence source,
freshness admission, rights posture, or permitted use.

## Audit Finding

The prior Market Update surface reused Market Preparation evidence correctly,
but rendered the producer's mechanical display labels directly and assembled
its summaries from generic string templates. The result preserved caution but
did not consistently distinguish raw facts, definitions, synthesis,
conversation relevance, and verification needs.

## Narrative Contract

Every rendered Market Update narrative statement has one explicit class:

- `DIRECT_OBSERVATION`
- `DEFINITIONAL_EXPLANATION`
- `RELATIONAL_SYNTHESIS`
- `BOUNDED_PROFESSIONAL_QUESTION`
- `AUDIENCE_AWARE_EXPLANATION`
- `LIMITATION_VERIFICATION`

Each metric observation retains the admitted evidence ID, source identity,
market, source date, ATLAS observed date, freshness, and professional
verification status. The new narrative layer does not modify or enrich the
underlying source state.

## Metric Semantics

Active inventory is presented as active listings in the admitted snapshot.
Days on market remains a generic measure unless the evidence separately
certifies its aggregation or methodology. Median price remains a generic
median-price measure unless the evidence separately certifies whether it is a
list, sold, or other price definition.

## Intelligence Boundary

The synthesis may connect selected inventory, days-on-market, and price
measures as different views of one dated snapshot. It may not infer a trend,
market temperature, buyer or seller leverage, affordability, causation,
forecast, pricing recommendation, offer strategy, negotiation strategy,
investment advice, suitability conclusion, or property-specific conclusion.

Audience and purpose change the explanation, questions, talking points, and
next actions, but do not change the admitted evidence or authorize an outcome.

## Session And Authorization Boundary

The capability remains Agent-only, read-only, session-only, unaddressed, and
unsent. It creates no customer record, recipient selection, CRM task, email,
SMS, publication, provider activity, database persistence, Admin inheritance,
or external action. Private Development Access and `HUMAN_AGENT_SESSION`
remain unchanged.

## Future Boundaries

`PROJECT_ATLAS_COMPARATIVE_MARKET_REPORTING_AND_EXPORT_REQUIRED` remains a
separate future requirement. This work introduces no multi-market comparison,
period calculation, chart, report builder, print layout, or export.

`PROJECT_ATLAS_VISUAL_ORIENTATION_AND_CAPABILITY_DIFFERENTIATION_REQUIRED`
also remains separate. The previously certified selection hierarchy is
preserved.
