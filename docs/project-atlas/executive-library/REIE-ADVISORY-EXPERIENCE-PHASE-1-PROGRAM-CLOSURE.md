## 3. Product Purpose

The Advisory Experience is the final step of the REIE Decision Journey.

It helps customers:

- move from REIE research into a prepared professional conversation;
- organize what they already know;
- identify unresolved questions;
- understand what requires professional verification;
- understand what happens next;
- prepare without pressure.

It is not:

- a generic Contact page;
- a CRM intake workflow;
- a scheduling product;
- a lead-scoring system;
- a persistent workspace;
- an automated qualification tool;
- an AI advisor;
- a recommendation engine;
- a professional-advice substitute.
