## Governance

Unknown city imagery fails closed. A city-specific image cannot become public eligible until the asset has confirmed ownership or license, attribution requirements, permitted public display, editorial approval, and fallback behavior.

This inventory does not authorize external image acquisition, scraping, upload, CDN mutation, image provider activation, or customer-facing replacement.

## Future Requirements

1. Asset-level source or owner.
2. License or ownership record.
3. Permitted public display statement.
4. Attribution text where required.
5. Editorial approval.
6. Accessibility-ready alt text.
7. Fallback asset.
8. Expiration or renewal handling where applicable.
