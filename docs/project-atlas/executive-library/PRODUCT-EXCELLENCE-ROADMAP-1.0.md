## 1. Executive Vision

PROJECT ATLAS has entered the Product Excellence Phase.

The enterprise architecture foundation is mature enough that the next 6 to 18 months should optimize the customer-facing Real Estate Intelligence Engine rather than continue architecture work by default.

The product vision:

> A Colorado buyer or seller should feel, within five minutes, that David Quinn Group operates with unusual clarity, intelligence, taste, discipline, and local authority.

The roadmap should maximize:

- customer trust
- perceived quality
- luxury experience
- business value
- market differentiation
- implementation leverage

The strongest product posture is not more visible complexity. It is a calmer, more complete, more authoritative customer journey that makes the platform feel premium and useful before a customer ever submits a form.

## 2. Product Philosophy

The REIE should feel like a high-trust advisory product, not a generic lead-generation site.

After five minutes, a customer should feel:

- oriented rather than sold to
- confident that the experience is professionally maintained
- aware that search, property, market, seller, and advisory paths belong to one coherent system
- impressed by the intelligence layer without feeling overwhelmed
- comfortable moving toward a consultation because the next step is clear and low-friction

After five minutes, a customer should believe:

- David Quinn Group understands Colorado real estate at a deeper level than a standard search portal
- the team values preparation, context, and judgment
- the site is premium because it is useful, restrained, and trustworthy
- customer decisions will be treated carefully
- claims are bounded by evidence, source, and professional discipline

Product excellence therefore means:

- fewer unfinished pathways
- more confidence at every transition
- visible intelligence that remains understandable
- premium restraint rather than decoration
- customer action paths that are clear without becoming aggressive

## 3. Customer Experience Themes

### Trust

Trust comes from consistent identity, clear navigation, preserved brokerage disclosure, honest source boundaries, no unsupported claims, and protected operational behavior.

Current maturity: high after REIE 7.1 Sprint 1 certification.

Next product need: remove remaining trust gaps caused by unfinished requested routes and unresolved compliance-dependent experiences.

### Discovery

Discovery should help customers move from Home to Search, Property, Market, Seller, Grand Plan, About, and Contact without wondering where they are.

Current maturity: high for certified routes.

Next product need: continue route completion only where customer value and governance readiness are strong.

### Luxury

Luxury should be expressed through restraint, spacing, typography, hierarchy, high-quality media, and confidence in what is not shown.

Current maturity: materially improved by Sprint 1, but still open for future page-level polish.

Next product need: apply luxury standards to high-value new routes instead of broad redesign.

### Search

Search is certified and customer-useful. It should remain stable.

Current maturity: high.

Next product need: map visual theme work may improve delight but depends on provider/style feasibility and should not outrank route-completion opportunities.

### Buying

Buying is strong through search and property intelligence but incomplete around financing.

Current maturity: medium-high.

Next product need: buyer financing readiness review before any mortgage calculator or lender page implementation.

### Selling

Selling has a certified `/sell` journey and valuation intake, but the requested dedicated home-worth route remains incomplete.

Current maturity: high foundation, incomplete route clarity.

Next product need: dedicated seller valuation route using existing valuation posture and without automated valuation claims.

### Financing

Financing can create high lead-generation and retention value, but it has compliance, disclosure, assumptions, and lender-boundary dependencies.

Current maturity: low for public route completion.

Next product need: readiness review before runtime implementation.

### Market Intelligence

Market intelligence is certified and differentiating.

Current maturity: high.

Next product need: maintain and connect into seller and financing decisions without inventing forecasts.

### Editorial Authority

Editorial authority can differentiate the platform through local expertise, but Sundance and article expansion require content ownership, route purpose, rights, and editorial governance.

Current maturity: medium; article framework exists, Sundance content does not.

Next product need: local editorial readiness after higher-conversion route gaps.

### Personalization

Personalization should remain deferred until measurement, consent, privacy, and customer-data governance justify it.

Current maturity: governed readiness only.

Next product need: no activation until CIM consent and business measurement decisions are separately authorized.

### Mobile Excellence

Mobile comfort is essential to perceived quality.

Current maturity: high for Sprint 1 reviewed routes after production certification.

Next product need: require every future sprint to certify narrow mobile behavior before closure.
