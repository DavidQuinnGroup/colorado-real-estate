# REIE Module 6 Financial Scenario Presentation Policy MVV Certification

Status: `REIE_MODULE_6_FINANCIAL_SCENARIO_PRESENTATION_POLICY_MVV_CERTIFIED_LOCAL`

The presentation policy allowlists assumption inventory, bounded arithmetic
illustrations, recurring-cost and category preparation, factual scenario
comparison, missing-input lists, and professional questions. Derived arithmetic
must expose its inputs, assumptions, calculation type, and limitations.

The policy rejects the complete prohibited taxonomy covering approval,
qualification, affordability, buying power, current rates, lender outputs,
suitability, tax/legal/investment/retirement advice, valuation and forecasts,
net-proceeds conclusions, cash-out, sale probability, offer, concession,
negotiation, and personalized financial strategy. Recommendations are always
false. Unknown kinds and prohibited classifications fail closed.
