## 7. Evidence Classification Standard

Certified definitions:

- `Available now`
- `Needs verification`
- `Not confirmed here`
- `Assumption`
- `Unknown from current evidence`

Exact copy uniformity is not required. Route-specific language is appropriate where route decisions differ.

## 8. Qualitative-Confidence Standard

Qualitative confidence means preparation-focused evidence confidence. It may address completeness, freshness, verification posture, assumption visibility, and limitation severity. It must not become a score, grade, approval, valuation, suitability label, prediction, ranking, recommendation, lead score, or hidden composite.

## 9. Verification Standard

Verification remains route-specific:

- Search: criteria and inventory review.
- Property: source records, inspection, title, HOA, insurance, taxes, permits, systems, financing assumptions, and contract review.
- Buyer: lender, financing, tax, insurance, HOA, title, inspection, property condition, and professional review.
- Seller: condition, records, pricing strategy, market alternatives, tax, legal, title, disclosure, insurance, and advisor review.
- Market and City Market: source, freshness, directional context, property-level verification, and professional interpretation.
- Neighborhood: place orientation, address-level diligence, insurance, access assumptions, records, and fair-housing boundaries.
- Advisory: evidence reviewed, assumptions remaining, questions requiring professional discussion, and professional boundaries.
- Contact: route choice, minimum useful information, privacy, consent, brokerage, and professional boundaries.

## 10. Threshold Standard
