## 6. Source-Rights Model

Governed source-rights states:

- public display permitted
- attribution-required public display
- derived or summarized use
- internal analysis only
- restricted
- unknown
- unresolved
- prohibited

Certified findings:

- unknown rights do not establish public-use authority.
- unresolved rights do not establish public-use authority.
- restricted evidence is not publicly eligible.
- prohibited evidence is blocked.
- attribution obligations are preserved.
- display rights and derivative-use rights remain distinct.
- internal-analysis rights do not imply public-display rights.
- acquisition permission does not imply use permission.
- evidence possession is not treated as publishing authority.

This closure records governance posture only and does not provide legal conclusions.

## 7. Support-Level And Freshness Models

Support levels:

- unsupported
- contextual
- corroborative
- direct
- authoritative

These categories describe evidence relationship only. They do not rank markets, rank evidence quality, establish certainty, create scores, create percentages, or create weighted conclusions.

Freshness states:

- current
- aging
- stale
- undated
- not applicable

Freshness is evaluated through domain-aware or injected policy. Undated evidence cannot appear current. Stale evidence carries limitations. No live freshness retrieval occurs. No universal unsupported threshold was imposed across all domains.

## 8. Conflict, Supersession, And Lineage

Conflict states:

- no known conflict
- compatible evidence
- unresolved conflict
- materially conflicting evidence
- superseded evidence
- insufficient information to reconcile

Certified conflict findings:

- conflicts are preserved.
- evidence is not silently collapsed.
- no source is automatically selected as the winner.
- relevant identities, versions, dates, rights, and limitations remain represented.
- no hidden weighting or source-prestige resolution was introduced.

Supported lineage relationships:

- supersedes
- superseded by
- derived from
- corroborates
- conflicts with
- remains historically valid

Prior evidence versions remain preserved. Supersession does not erase history. Replacement applies only within a defined purpose or period. Evidence lineage remains inspectable through the contract.

## 9. Public-Use Eligibility And Limitations

Deterministic public-use eligibility outcomes:

- eligible
- eligible with limitations
- internal only
- blocked
- unresolved

Eligibility considers source rights, permitted use, attribution, freshness, provenance completeness, support level, conflict state, structured limitations, and domain restrictions. Eligibility fails closed.

Structured limitations include:

- stale evidence
- undated evidence
- uncertain rights
- unresolved conflict
- incomplete geographic coverage
- incomplete temporal coverage
- indirect evidence
- citywide versus property-specific limitation
- professional verification required
- non-public-use restriction

Limitations describe evidence constraints and do not establish conclusions about a property, customer, community, neighborhood, city, market, value, condition, suitability, investment quality, or professional opinion.
