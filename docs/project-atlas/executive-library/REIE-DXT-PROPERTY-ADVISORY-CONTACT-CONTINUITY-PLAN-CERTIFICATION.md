## Responsibility Contract

The certified future continuity model preserves distinct route responsibilities:

- Property evaluates the specific property.
- Advisory prepares the professional conversation.
- Contact begins a general conversation.
- Property inquiry remains the property-specific information path.

The plan does not merge Property inquiry into generic Contact, does not make Advisory a form, and does not make Contact property-specific through hidden context.

## Certified Planning Findings

The plan confirms:

- Property should continue to Advisory when the customer needs preparation for a broader professional conversation.
- Property should use Property inquiry when the customer has a specific question about the property.
- Generic Contact remains appropriate for direct conversation starts.
- Visible property context may be considered only under separate runtime authorization.
- Direct Property, Advisory, and Contact entry must remain understandable without prior journey state.
- Duplicate equal-priority actions should be reduced route-by-route, not through a shared runtime abstraction in this phase.
