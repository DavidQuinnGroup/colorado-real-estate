# Market Product 3.0 Future VIS Adoption Findings

Status: `MARKET_PRODUCT_3_FUTURE_VIS_FINDINGS_COMPLETE`

## Findings

Market Product 3.0 proves that Visual Intelligence can be adopted publicly when it uses existing governed runtime data, explicit trust boundaries, and bounded evidence states.

## High-Value Future Candidates

- Property Product: Property DNA and Confidence Layer after separate authorization.
- Search Product: result explanation and confidence framing after separate authorization.
- Decision Guides: certified city report composition after editorial certification.
- Neighborhood Product: local evidence confidence only after source rights and persistence are governed.

## Deferred Candidates

- Inventory Horizon: deferred until period-over-period market data and freshness rules are governed.
- Public GIS: deferred until provider activation, rights, persistence, and public-display rules are authorized.
- Source-backed local evidence: deferred until Boulder County Open Data or another source clears rights, counsel, persistence, and activation gates.

## Boundaries

- No AI
- No public GIS
- No telemetry
- No new providers
- No source activation
- No schema or Prisma changes
- No public incomplete intelligence
