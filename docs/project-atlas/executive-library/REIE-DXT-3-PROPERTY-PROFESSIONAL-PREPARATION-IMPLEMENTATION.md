## Governing Question

After evaluating this property, what should I organize before asking a property-specific question or beginning a focused professional conversation?

## Implementation Summary

The Property route now includes one concise `Property Professional Preparation` layer. It organizes existing public Property evidence, evidence still needed, assumptions, unknowns, questions to verify, conversation priorities, and pathway choice without changing Property Inquiry, Advisory, Contact, Search, APIs, forms, persistence, telemetry, CRM, email, scheduling, navigation, footer, or brokerage disclosure.

## Evidence Treatment

- Evidence reviewed: existing visible listing facts, Property DNA, Product 3.1, Property Decision Readiness, comparison basis, Market pathway, condition and construction prompts, financial and transaction prompts, Search-return context, related listings, and existing professional handoff.
- Evidence still needed: condition, costs, records, inspections, permits, title, disclosures, HOA, insurance, legal, tax, lending, appraisal, contract, and other professional review categories.
- Assumptions: public listing facts are orientation only; calculated metrics use listed price and square footage only; market context is directional; professional review remains separate.
- Unknowns: condition, systems, records, disclosures, total ownership costs, comparable interpretation, timing, and local context remain unconfirmed.
- Confidence: qualitative and limitation-forward only; no score, prediction, recommendation, suitability label, valuation, or ranking is introduced.

## Question And Priority Treatment

Static conversation-priority categories are used:

- Property facts.
- Condition and inspections.
- Financing assumptions.
- Market context.
- Title, HOA, or transaction.
- General professional preparation.

These categories are not saved choices, hidden state, customer profiles, form prefills, CRM classifications, telemetry events, lead scores, recommendations, risk findings, or professional conclusions.

## Pathway Hierarchy

1. Property Inquiry remains dominant for property-specific questions.
2. Advisory remains the preparation path before a focused professional conversation.
3. Contact remains the subordinate general conversation path.

Destinations preserved:

- Property Inquiry: `#property-contact`
- Advisory: `/contact#advisory-readiness`
- Contact: `/contact#contact-route-choice`
