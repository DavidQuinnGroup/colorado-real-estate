# REIE Boulder Permit Exact Source Registry MVV Certification

## Scope

This MVV adds three exact Source Registry identities for separately governable permit channels:

- City of Boulder Open Data permit/planning exports;
- City of Boulder building-permit and inspection portal;
- Boulder County Community Planning & Permitting / Accela permit portal.

The existing `SRC-BOULDER-PERMIT-CANDIDATES` record remains a verification prompt and discovery-only context. It is not an aggregate, parent evidence authority, or Source Quality evidence record.

## Registry posture

Each new record is `AUTHORITATIVE_SOURCE`, `BUILDING_PERMITS`, `AWAITING_PROVIDER_CONFIRMATION`, `BLOCKED_NOT_AUTHORIZED`, and claim-ineligible. Registry identity does not certify rights, technical access, freshness, attribution, provenance, retrieval, customer display, or legal use.

City Open Data language does not imply unrestricted reuse, redistribution, automation, or currentness. Portal existence does not imply approved automation, scraping permission, API stability, or display authority. City and County channels remain separate and no cross-source inference is permitted.

## Candidate firewall

The candidate record remains verification-only with no retrieval/display and no candidate-to-member, parent-to-child, or aggregate relationship semantics. No relationship model was added.

## Source Quality firewall

This is Registry identity architecture only. No permit Source Quality evidence, County Conversion support, Human-Reviewed finding, Operational Manifest entry, activation, retrieval, or customer-display authority is created.

## Evidence basis

The records use only structured repository-local source identity and readiness context. No external research, portal access, correspondence, protected county artifact, permit data, PII, credentials, or provider call was used.
