# PROJECT ATLAS(tm)

## Enterprise Implementation Program(tm) - Sprint 4 Lessons Learned

Status: `EIP_1.0_SPRINT_4_INTERNAL_GEOGRAPHIC_ACTIVATION_READINESS_LEDGER_CERTIFIED_AND_CLOSED`

Implementation date: July 25, 2026

---

## 1. Quality Is Evidence, Not Authority

Sprint 4 confirmed that quality results are useful inputs, but they cannot become activation authority.

A high-quality internal record can be ready for executive review while still being not authorized and not active.

---

## 2. Internal Proof Completion Is Historical Accounting

Sprint 1 and Sprint 2 proof stages can be marked internally complete because persistence proof and retrieval proof already occurred in an isolated deterministic workflow.

That completion does not authorize production persistence, customer visibility, property relationships, search, maps, indexing, analytics, public pages, or AI-assisted synthesis.

---

## 3. Readiness Must Be Gate-Specific

The same knowledge object can be complete for internal retrieval, incomplete for mapping, blocked for customer presentation, and ready only for executive review on another gate.

One global readiness state would hide important governance distinctions.

---

## 4. Ambiguity And Conflict Need Different Handling

Object-type ambiguity should route to human review. Preserved conflict and duplicate evidence should block canonical or customer-facing activation.

Sprint 4 made that distinction explicit in the ledger.

---

## 5. The Next Valuable Step Is Decision Packaging

The platform now has internal memory, retrieval, quality assessment, and readiness accounting.

Sprint 5 should not activate anything. The next useful increment is an internal executive review packet that groups ledger evidence into clear proposed decisions, blockers, tradeoffs, and recommended sequencing.
