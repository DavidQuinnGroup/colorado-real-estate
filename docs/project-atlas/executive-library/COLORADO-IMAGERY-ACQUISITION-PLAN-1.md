# Colorado Imagery Acquisition Plan(tm) 1.0

Status: `COLORADO_IMAGERY_ACQUISITION_PLAN_CREATED`

Date: July 29, 2026

## Executive Summary

The fastest lawful path to premium Colorado city imagery is a DQG-owned or commissioned photography program with asset-level rights documentation. Unknown-rights imagery remains prohibited for public use and must fail closed to approved fallback imagery.

## Acquisition Channels

| Channel | Speed | Rights Confidence | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DQG photography program | Fastest | High | Primary path for priority Colorado city hero and neighborhood imagery |
| Commissioned photography | Fast | High | Use work-for-hire or explicit web-use agreements for city gaps |
| Municipal/public-domain imagery | Medium | Medium | Use only after terms, attribution, modification, and commercial-web rights review |
| Licensed stock/editorial imagery | Fast | Medium | Secondary option with license, crop, expiration, and attribution tracking |
| Fallback imagery | Fastest | High | Continue until city-specific rights are confirmed |

## Quality Requirements

- Colorado-first visual identity.
- Accurate city/location relationship.
- No misleading location implication.
- No identifiable private individuals without release.
- No private interiors without permission.
- Strong mobile crops.
- Accessibility-ready alt text.
- Source, owner, license, attribution, approval, renewal, and fallback recorded.

## Fastest Lawful Plan

1. Build shot list for Boulder, Louisville, Lafayette, Erie, Longmont, and Superior.
2. Capture DQG-owned or commissioned exterior/community imagery.
3. Record ownership/release, location, subject, alt text, and approval.
4. Use fallback imagery until each city asset is public eligible.
5. Track renewals only for licensed third-party assets.

This plan is not legal advice and does not issue final legal conclusions.
