## Professional Playbook Contract

Buyer Preparation preserves the concise Executive Briefing as the first
approximately 60-second review. After the Agent chooses to prepare or update
the briefing, it also produces a progressive consultation playbook covering
Buyer position and readiness, discovery questions, financial readiness, search
strategy, neutral City context, admitted Market posture, property evaluation,
process roadmap, offer-preparation education, professional checkpoints,
representation/process questions, plan changes, agenda, and next actions.

Selected consultation topics control visible emphasis without excluding the
surrounding process context required for a coherent consultation. The playbook
is assembled only after the existing same-page prepare/update action and is not
part of initial route work.

## Certified Intelligence Reuse

The playbook composes the existing Agent Buyer admission and briefing contract,
certified Agent Place conversation context, Buyer financing readiness boundary,
offer-preparation readiness boundary, professional handoff cohesion, and Buyer
journey/process terminology. It does not manufacture current Market facts.
When Market evidence is not admitted, the playbook renders the limitation and
directs the Agent to separate Market Preparation before relying on it.

Louisville renders as neutral City orientation only. City context does not
become neighborhood, property, demographic, safety, school, or suitability
guidance; property and address-specific facts remain verification work.

## Professional Boundaries

Financial material is classified as Agent process education, client-reported
context, a question to ask, or lender verification. The playbook makes no
affordability, qualification, approval, underwriting, rate, loan-product,
lender-selection, legal, tax, representation-requirement, valuation, offer,
negotiation, suitability, or protected-class conclusion.

Professional checkpoints organize questions for lenders, inspectors or
engineers, title, insurance, tax, legal, and municipal or county authorities.
They do not recommend a provider, create a referral relationship, or contact
any professional.

## Continuity and Action Discipline

Buyer inputs remain visible, editable, session-only, and same-page. Existing
exact-route Place, Property, and where applicable Market continuation actions
remain available once in the Buyer Next Action Plan. They carry no query,
fragment, hidden state, or cross-capability input transfer. Each destination
continues under the existing Human Agent session and evaluates its own admitted
context.
