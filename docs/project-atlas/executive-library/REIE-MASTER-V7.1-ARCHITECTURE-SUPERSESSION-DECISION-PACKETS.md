# REIE Master V7.1 Architecture And Supersession Decision Packets

Program: PROJECT ATLAS / REIE Product Experience, Capability, and Master-Vision Reconciliation
Date: 2026-08-18
Status: `EXECUTIVE_DECISION_PREPARATION_ONLY`

## Decision Rule

These packets prepare Executive choices. They do not select an option, mark a
Master requirement superseded, authorize implementation, or authorize a
customer-facing visual, route, provider, financial, persistence, or production
change. Any selected option must be recorded against the Four-Document
Governing Check in
`PROJECT-ATLAS-COMMAND-AND-EXECUTION-LOCATION-STANDARD.md`.

Each packet uses one recommended disposition:

`KEEP_AND_COMPLETE`, `KEEP_BUT_REARCHITECT`, `FULFILLED_BY_CERTIFIED_SUCCESSOR`,
`PARTIALLY_FULFILLED_BY_SUCCESSOR`, `DEPRECATE_LEGACY_IMPLEMENTATION`,
`INTERNAL_ONLY`, `COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `DATA_SOURCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`,
or `EXECUTIVE_DECISION_REQUIRED`.

## Packet 1: Module 6 Advanced Lending And Investment Scope

**Decision question:** Should Module 6 remain a bounded educational and
question-preparation capability, or should Executive HQ authorize a separately
governed financial product with lending, investment, source, and compliance
controls?

**Current evidence:** Buyer Financing Planner, user-assumption scenario
calculator, FHA/conventional calculation foundations, TCOL/CAPEX foundations,
financial narrative code, and Advisory preparation exist. There is no complete
Digital Loan Officer, investment hub, live-rate source, lender workflow,
portfolio suitability engine, or Sundance Yield Model.

**Recommended disposition:** `KEEP_BUT_REARCHITECT` for educational financing;
`COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` for investment, lender, yield, profitability,
appreciation, and strategic narrative scope.

**Required decision:** Define permitted educational outputs, prohibited
recommendations, source/freshness requirements, professional handoff, and
whether investment/yield concepts are deprecated.

## Packet 2: Module 7 Senior Transitions

**Decision question:** Is Senior Transitions a distinct product Hub, a bounded
neutral orientation layer, or a deferred Master concept?

**Current evidence:** Generic seller, home-worth, financial, moving, property,
CRM, document-adjacent, and Advisory foundations exist. No senior-specific
route, facility source, care filter, Medicare/tax guardrail, heir invitation,
or document vault was found.

**Recommended disposition:** `EXECUTIVE_DECISION_REQUIRED`, with any retained
scope requiring `COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and `DATA_SOURCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

**Required decision:** Confirm audience, permitted neutral questions, care and
financial boundaries, identity/consent model, document-retention posture, and
whether the Hub remains in the product roadmap.

## Packet 3: Module 8 Unified Suite Versus Integration Layer

**Decision question:** Should the Multi-Dimensional Strategy Suite become one
named product surface, or remain an orchestration layer over Buyer, Seller,
Market, Property, Financing, Grand Plan, and Advisory products?

**Current evidence:** The repository contains distributed decision workspaces,
Grand Plan, compare, seller preparation, offer preparation, logistics, and
Advisory handoff. No unified Suite contract or route was found.

**Recommended disposition:** `PARTIALLY_FULFILLED_BY_SUCCESSOR` as an
integration/orchestration layer pending Executive confirmation.

**Required decision:** Set ownership of cross-journey context, define whether
state remains explicit and user-controlled, and identify the single customer
decision that would justify a unified surface.

## Packet 4: Module 10 Remaining MCP Logic

**Decision question:** Does the current internal Master Control Panel provide
functional equivalence to the supplied Master Module 10, or is a narrower
operational MCP the certified successor?

**Current evidence:** `/admin` and `MasterControlPanel` provide protected
operational visibility and control state, including mode, public exposure, map
precision, private layer, strategy gate, and kill switch. Exact valuation-detail,
lending-scenario, Reveal Protocol, and 40-60 semantics were not established.

**Recommended disposition:** `INTERNAL_ONLY` for the current operational MCP;
`EXECUTIVE_DECISION_REQUIRED` for remaining Master logic.

**Required decision:** Define canonical toggles, ownership, audit requirements,
map privacy semantics, and whether any reveal behavior is retained.

## Packet 6: Recommended Lender Page

**Decision question:** Should REIE create a Recommended Lender page, and if so,
what relationship, disclosure, selection, and compliance model governs it?

**Current evidence:** No lender route exists. Buyer financing surfaces direct
customers to questions for qualified professionals and do not rank or

**Recommended disposition:** `COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` before any route or
partner content work.

**Required decision:** Determine whether the page is informational, a referral,
or an affiliated-business arrangement; identify approvals, disclosures,
selection criteria, compensation, and customer-data boundaries.

## Decision Packet Exit Criteria

An Executive packet is ready for a future decision when it has:

- governing-document consultation recorded;
- current certified successor identified;
- exact repository evidence and unknowns separated;
- customer, admin, agent, and provider boundaries stated;
- compliance and source questions named;
- selected disposition explicitly approved;
- implementation authorization issued separately, if needed.
