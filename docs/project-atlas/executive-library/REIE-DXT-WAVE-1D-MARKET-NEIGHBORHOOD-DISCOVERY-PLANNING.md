## 4. Duplicated Presentation Patterns

Observed duplicated or adjacent patterns:

- market and neighborhood pages both introduce place context, property discovery, Search continuation, and decision-guide continuity;
- Market Product 3 and Neighborhood Product 3 both carry visual-intelligence style interpretation patterns;
- city and neighborhood pages both expose market stats, neighborhood link groups, Search links, financing education, trust boundaries, and FAQ/schema support;
- cards and link groups can compete for attention when several continuation destinations appear in the same viewport;
- evidence, confidence, and visual-intelligence sections may repeat concepts before the customer chooses whether they need market-level or neighborhood-level context.

These are planning observations only. They are not defect findings and do not authorize runtime remediation.

## 5. Customer Decision Flow

Recommended future customer flow:

1. choose the scope of place context: statewide, city, or neighborhood;
2. understand the governing decision question for that scope;
3. see the strongest current signal and its limitation;
4. continue to Search if property discovery is the next step;
5. continue to neighborhood detail when local fit needs more context;
6. continue to seller or buyer guidance when the decision is preparation rather than place comparison;
7. keep evidence and professional boundaries adjacent to claims;
8. close with compact next-decision routes.

Market pages should answer:

What place-level context should shape my next search, buyer, or seller decision?

Neighborhood pages should answer:

What local context should I verify before treating this neighborhood as a serious fit?
