## 14. Incident Classification

### Severity 1: Launch-Critical

Examples:

- homepage unavailable;
- Search unavailable;
- material map or property discovery failure;
- Contact or Advisory unavailable;
- unsafe public claims;
- route, canonical, or sitemap corruption;
- unauthorized production mutation;
- deployment mismatch.

Decision rule:

- launch must pause, be suspended, or enter rollback review;
- Executive Launch Owner, Technical Verification Owner, Product Verification Owner, Monitoring Owner, and Rollback Authority must be notified;
- evidence must be recorded before any completion declaration.

### Severity 2: Material But Non-Critical

Examples:

- isolated route defect outside launch-critical path;
- responsive issue on one surface that does not block launch-critical tasks;
- broken secondary continuation;
- non-critical console issue.

Decision rule:

- Support Owner classifies and tracks;
- Product or Technical Verification Owner determines whether it can be deferred;
- repeated Severity 2 issues may be escalated to Severity 1.

### Severity 3: Post-Launch Improvement

Examples:

- visual refinement;
- copy polish;
- minor spacing issue;
- non-critical enhancement request.

Decision rule:

- classify as post-launch;
- do not interrupt launch unless combined issues create material risk;
- require a later authorization gate before implementation.
