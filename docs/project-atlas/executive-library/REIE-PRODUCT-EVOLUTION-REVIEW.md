## 4. Product Evolution Findings

The product now has a stronger entry and a richer buyer-financing decision-support surface, but the customer-facing advisory endpoint has not been revisited after those additions.

Repository evidence shows:

- Homepage Phase 1 resolved the primary homepage density, hierarchy, Communities-grid, Search-entry, mobile, whitespace, and bordered-container problems.
- Buyer Financing Decision Planner Phase 1 added a bounded in-page decision planner inside `/buy#financing-readiness`.
- Advisory Handoff is certified as the endpoint for decision context, readiness exits, questions-to-bring, and professional-boundary framing.
- Advisory Operating Readiness is certified internally as a standard for consistent advisor preparation without customer data, CRM, personalization, automation, or conclusions.
- The current `/contact` page is still primarily a Public Trust and routing page with an embedded Advisory Handoff surface. That surface is governed and useful, but it predates the newly closed financing planner and the homepage simplification direction.

The strongest current leverage is not another isolated calculator, route, city, Search, or market feature. It is a planning pass that defines how advisory readiness should feel and function as the customer-facing endpoint for the now-certified journey system.

## 5. Candidates Evaluated

### Homepage Product Experience Phase 2

Disposition: defer.

Customer value is moderate, but Phase 1 already resolved the primary homepage problem. Repository closure records show improved first-screen purpose, reduced density, removed Communities grid, meaningful whitespace, CTA-led Search, and responsive certification. Phase 2 visual refinement should wait for a concrete post-Phase 1 friction finding or a reusable component need.

### Destination-Page Extraction

Disposition: defer.

Homepage Phase 1 preserved governed journeys without stranding necessary content. No repository record currently identifies a specific homepage section that must become a new route. Route creation would add SEO, navigation, sitemap, canonical, and maintenance obligations without a current evidence-backed need.

### Reusable Product Experience Design System

Disposition: defer.

Enterprise leverage is high, but the risk is broad site-wide change without a concrete defect. Homepage-scoped principles are certified, and the Buyer Financing Planner reused restrained product principles locally. A broader system should follow repeated component-level need across multiple surfaces or a separate design-system planning authorization.

### Buyer Journey Expansion

Disposition: defer.

Buyer Financing Decision Planner Phase 1 just closed the most concrete buyer journey gap. Further buyer expansion should wait for production evidence from the certified planner or a specific unresolved buyer decision that is not already handled by Search, Buy, Financing Readiness, Grand Plan, or Advisory.

### Seller Journey Expansion

Disposition: defer.

Seller Readiness, Home Worth, Property / Seller Evidence, and Advisory Handoff are certified. Seller expansion remains attractive but higher risk because it can drift toward valuation, pricing, net proceeds, condition conclusions, public-record lookup, provider evidence, or customer-specific seller profiles. No current repository record identifies a single seller-surface defect stronger than advisory productization.

### Search Refinement

Disposition: defer.

Search remains central, but no verified Search defect, unsafe behavior, broken continuity, ranking issue, or production friction is recorded in the current authorization context. Search work should be selected only with concrete production or repository evidence.

### Market Intelligence Enhancement

Disposition: defer.

Market and neighborhood surfaces have recently received bounded geographic work. Niwot is non-public, Gunbarrel remains unresolved, and Local Decision Intelligence Wave 4 is blocked. Additional market intelligence could create route, registry, Search, map/GIS, source-rights, or geography-governance risk without a current customer-facing defect.

### Advisory Experience Enhancement

Disposition: selected for bounded planning.

Customer value is high because Advisory is the natural endpoint after Search, market, neighborhood, buyer, financing, seller, Grand Plan, comparison, and evidence-aware exploration. Enterprise leverage is high because it can reuse Product Cohesion, Decision Journey, Advisory Handoff, Advisory Operating Readiness, Buyer Financing Planner, Seller Readiness, and Evidence Depth without creating new data systems.

The work should be planning-first because implementation boundaries are sensitive: no CRM, no scheduling, no lead scoring, no hidden context transfer, no persistence, no automation, no professional advice, no new route unless planning proves one is necessary.

### Local Decision Intelligence Prerequisite Work

Disposition: defer.

LDI Wave 4 remains blocked by candidate readiness. Repository records show no candidate currently satisfies combined governance, route, registry, Search, map/GIS, evidence, provenance, source-rights, and public-maturity requirements. Returning to geographic prerequisites now would repeat a blocked track.

### Deliberate Pause

Disposition: rejected.

A pause is not the highest-value choice because the certified portfolio now exposes a concrete strategic question: how the advisory endpoint should absorb and clarify the full decision journey after the homepage and financing planner improvements. Planning can proceed without implementation or protected-system activation.
