# Boulder County Open Data Provider Confirmation Request 1

Purpose: prepare a dataset-specific provider request for Boulder County. This is not legal advice and does not authorize activation.

## Requested Datasets

- Address Points, catalog item `687530b74ad54686a98f50337574596f`
- Boulder County Parks and Open Space Park Boundaries, catalog item `ffbeca86d075420cafc960bba6e5d4e8`
- Boulder County Open Space, catalog item `0ff5754576af44cbb0fddaf1995b767a`
- Traffic Stations, catalog item `aeb2b2385dec4ffea0320318e3f2248a`

## Questions

1. Confirm that each named catalog item is Boulder County-authorized for third-party reuse.
2. Confirm whether catalog-level CC-BY-4.0 terms or each item license controls when the two differ.
3. Confirm required attribution wording for Boulder County, department, dataset title, license, and access date.
4. Confirm whether REIE may store a snapshot of selected public records internally.
5. Confirm whether REIE may transform source records into normalized internal observations.
6. Confirm whether REIE may aggregate records into city-level or neighborhood-level summaries.
7. Confirm whether derived summaries may be displayed to customers with attribution.
8. Confirm whether raw geometries may be stored internally, and whether public map display is restricted.
9. Confirm whether address, parcel, account, or tax district fields have field-level restrictions.
10. Confirm whether owner/person names, if present in future datasets, must be excluded.
11. Confirm export formats and supported API endpoints for scheduled use.
12. Confirm rate limits, acceptable automation cadence, and contact channel for operational issues.
13. Confirm whether credentials, accounts, contracts, or fees are required for any selected dataset.
14. Confirm source update cadence and whether changed-record deltas are available.
15. Confirm retention expectations for stale, corrected, deprecated, or removed records.
16. Confirm whether deletion or correction requests require downstream removal by REIE.
17. Confirm whether custom-license datasets can be used under the same terms as CC-BY catalog datasets.
18. Confirm required disclaimer language for approximate boundaries, data completeness, and no-warranty notices.
19. Confirm whether provider wants review of any public wording before launch.
20. Confirm escalation contacts for future source changes, deprecations, or license updates.
