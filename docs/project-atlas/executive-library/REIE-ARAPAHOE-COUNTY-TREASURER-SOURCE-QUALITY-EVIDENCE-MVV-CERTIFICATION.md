# REIE Arapahoe County Treasurer Source Quality Evidence MVV Certification

Certification ID: `CERT-ARAPAHOE-COUNTY-TREASURER-SOURCE-QUALITY-EVIDENCE-001`

## Scope

This certification creates source-specific Source Quality evidence for `SRC-ARAPAHOE-COUNTY-TREASURER`.

The evidence package is certification-reference-only and records no raw tax, property, parcel, payment, certificate, lien, owner, taxpayer, or person data.

## Firewalls

This evidence MVV does not authorize Tax Search automation, payment, daily or yearly extract use, tax statement or receipt retrieval, Certificate of Taxes Due purchase or use, delinquent-tax publication use, tax-lien operations, Public Trustee operations, Assessor use, Recorder use, GIS use, retrieval, customer display, redistribution, legal use, or runtime activation.

No Boulder Treasurer findings and no Arapahoe Assessor findings are inherited.
