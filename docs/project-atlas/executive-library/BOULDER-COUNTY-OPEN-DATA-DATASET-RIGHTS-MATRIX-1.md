# Boulder County Open Data Dataset Rights Matrix 1

This matrix is for provider and counsel review. It is not legal advice and does not authorize activation.

| Dataset | Catalog license posture | Storage | Transformation | Aggregation | Public display | Activation decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Address Points | CC-BY-4.0 record | Provider/counsel required | Provider/counsel required | Provider/counsel required | Field exclusions required | Review first |
| Park Boundaries | Custom license/disclaimer in record | Provider/counsel required | Provider/counsel required | Provider/counsel required | Approximate-boundary disclaimer required | Review first |
| Open Space | CC-BY-4.0 record | Provider/counsel required | Provider/counsel required | Provider/counsel required | Ownership/easement caveats required | Review second |
| Traffic Stations | CC-BY-4.0 record | Provider/counsel required | Provider/counsel required | Provider/counsel required | No commute/safety/ranking claims | Defer until clarified |
| Trail Segments Dissolved | CC-BY-4.0 visible in captured extract | Provider/counsel required | Provider/counsel required | Provider/counsel required | Closure/freshness caveats required | Defer until clarified |
| Emergency Alert Flood Polygons | CC-BY-4.0 record | Not approved | Not approved | Not approved | Not approved | Defer high sensitivity |
| Boulder Area Trailheads | CC-BY-4.0 record but deprecated | Not approved | Not approved | Not approved | Not approved | Defer deprecated/static |

## Prohibited Patterns

- Treating public access as final legal approval.
- Displaying raw address, parcel, account, owner, or hazard-adjacent records to customers.
- Creating neighborhood, safety, hazard, school, commute, investment, or desirability rankings.
- Activating public GIS layers.
- Writing provider data into production before a separately authorized persistence program.
