# REIE Source Quality Human-Reviewed Evidence Conversion Contract MVV Certification

## Scope

This contract accepts a human-supplied finite Source Quality finding and controlled metadata only. It does not open, parse, summarize, interpret, retrieve, or infer from source narrative.

## Core boundary

MODEL_DOES_NOT_PARSE_SOURCE_NARRATIVE. HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED. EXACT_SOURCE_ID_REQUIRED. FINITE_STRUCTURED_FINDING_REQUIRED. CONTROLLED_EVIDENCE_REFERENCE_REQUIRED. REVIEWED_AT_REQUIRED. CERTIFICATION_REQUIRED. NO_AUTOMATIC_RIGHTS_INFERENCE.

## Output

Compatible RIGHTS, TECHNICAL_ACCESS, FRESHNESS, ATTRIBUTION, and PROVENANCE findings yield canonical SourceEvidenceLinkageRecord values. FEE, DISCLAIMER, REFERRAL, and FOLLOW_UP remain finite governance findings accompanied by controlled certification linkage metadata. One finding does not populate unrelated dimensions.

## Firewalls

Narrative, correspondence, document bodies, web prose, chat content, PII, credentials, provider/county access, and protected reconciliation material are non-composable. Findings are evidence only; activation, retrieval, scraping, customer display, and legal use remain separately governed.

## Synthetic fixtures

All fixtures are synthetic governed metadata. They do not represent actual County, provider, Assessor, Treasurer, or rights findings.
