## Route Ownership Model

Property owns address-level evaluation and Property Inquiry.

Advisory owns focused professional-conversation preparation.

Contact owns general conversation initiation and path selection.

Buyer owns preparation to buy.

Seller owns preparation before market exposure.

Search owns inventory exploration and comparison context.

Market and City Market own broad and city-level evidence.

Neighborhood owns neutral place orientation.

No responsibility is merged across routes. Exact copy uniformity is not required when the customer decision differs.
