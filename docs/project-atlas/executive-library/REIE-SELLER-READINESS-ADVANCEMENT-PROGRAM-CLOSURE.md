## 7. Property-Context, Market-Context, And Advisory Guidance

Property-context prompts remain limitation-forward and cover topics such as:

- property age
- housing form
- known improvements
- deferred maintenance
- systems and components
- additions or conversions
- municipal or HOA requirements
- inspection, structural, environmental, insurance, title, or other specialist review where applicable

Market context is presented only as preparation information. It does not calculate or imply price, value, appreciation, demand, timing, urgency, or market superiority.

Advisory preparation helps a homeowner organize questions for a qualified real-estate conversation. The experience does not imply that REIE has evaluated the user's property or determined whether the owner should sell.
