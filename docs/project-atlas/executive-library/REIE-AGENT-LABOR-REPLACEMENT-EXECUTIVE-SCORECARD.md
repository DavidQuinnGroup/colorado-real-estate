## Methodology

The scorecard classifies agent-labor replacement from the row-level support state, maturity, labor intensity, frequency, source dependency, and retained-human-judgment fields in the Phase 1 matrix. It measures reduction or replacement of repetitive intelligence labor only. It does not measure fiduciary replacement, professional judgment replacement, legal/tax/lending/appraisal advice, negotiation, persuasion, physical inspection, or customer relationship work.

Scale:

- `HIGH_REPLACEMENT`: REIE materially performs repeatable research, organization, explanation, or evidence-routing work at production-certified or broadly implemented maturity.
- `MODERATE_REPLACEMENT`: REIE removes meaningful work, but manual verification, operational gaps, or uncertified pieces remain.
- `LOW_REPLACEMENT`: REIE has limited direct replacement effect.
- `INTELLIGENCE_AUGMENTATION`: REIE structures facts, questions, or preparation while humans execute the core work.
- `LARGELY_HUMAN`: the work should remain primarily human.
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`: repository evidence is inadequate for a replacement classification.

## High-Value Work Already Replaced

- Search, filtering, sorting, map search, price-band filtering, property-type filtering, bedroom/bath filtering, listing-status filtering, duplicate elimination, and search recovery/fallback.
- Visible property fact extraction, factual property summaries, missing-data identification, verification checklist preparation, source provenance, customer source transparency, and unavailable-evidence explanations.
- Customer decision structure, professional discussion prep, advisory handoff, decision continuity, false-certainty avoidance, and repeated city/neighborhood/source explanation.
- Team knowledge standardization around source documentation, methodology, verification standards, evidence boundaries, and professional-boundary reinforcement.

## High-Value Work Partially Supported

- Market briefing/report preparation, historical trend comparison, chart/data preparation, source reconciliation, content refresh, and customer talking-point preparation.
- Property due-diligence preparation, comparable research, sold-comp research, seller listing preparation, listing management, open-house preparation, and CRM/follow-up preparation.
- Source-rights review, public-record discovery, geographic governance, administrative quality checks, and production-readiness evidence assembly.

## High-Value Unsupported Work

- Production-authorized assessor, parcel, ownership, permit, zoning, flood/environmental, utility, and operational GIS/public-record retrieval.
- Legal advice, contract drafting, offer-price recommendation, acceptance-probability prediction, automated valuation/appraisal certainty, and physical networking/prospecting.
- Open-house scheduling/data handling, provider contact, human relationship building, and operational MLS mutation.

## Human-Value Work Retained

REIE should not attempt to replace trust and relationship formation, empathy, customer priority discovery, subjective preference interpretation, fiduciary judgment, negotiation, persuasion, strategy, physical observation, inspection judgment, contract/legal decisions, tax advice, lending advice, appraisal, ethical accountability, professional responsibility, communication nuance, or local lived experience. REIE's correct role is to remove repetitive intelligence labor so agents can concentrate on higher-value human work.

## Value Narrative

REIE currently gives an agent a reusable intelligence layer for search, property facts, city and neighborhood orientation, market context, source transparency, verification prompts, and customer decision preparation. It consolidates repeated research into visible routes and evidence-aware modules, standardizes how customers are oriented before conversations, and reduces duplicated work across agents by preserving source, freshness, and professional-boundary patterns. It does not turn those materials into advice, negotiation strategy, inspection judgment, valuation certainty, or legal/lending/tax conclusions. The practical value is a better prepared agent and customer: less repeated lookup work, more consistent factual context, clearer evidence limits, and more time left for human relationship, judgment, and strategy.

## Executive Recommendation

Treat Phase 1 as a completed executive inventory and scorecard, not as an implementation plan. The next useful gate is to choose one bounded, documentation-first candidate from the priority register for deeper feasibility and source/protected-boundary review before any runtime work.
