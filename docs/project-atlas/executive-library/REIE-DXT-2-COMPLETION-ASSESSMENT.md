## Cross-Route Terminology Standard

Certified documentation-level definitions:

- `Available now`: evidence or context already visible on the route and safe to rely on for orientation.
- `Needs verification`: material point requiring source, property, lender, market, or professional review before reliance.
- `Not confirmed here`: a route-local limitation stating that the current page does not verify the fact.
- `Assumption`: an explicit working premise treated as provisional.
- `Unknown from current evidence`: a material gap the current route cannot resolve.
- `Qualitative confidence`: preparation-focused confidence based on completeness, freshness, visible assumptions, verification posture, and limitation severity; never a score, approval, valuation, suitability label, or recommendation.
- `Freshness`: route-specific recency or currency posture without false precision.
- `Verification`: what should be checked through source records, property review, lender review, market review, or professional review.
- `Next-decision threshold`: a descriptive threshold for deciding what to inspect, compare, refine, verify, prepare, or discuss next; not a prescriptive decision.
- `Professional review`: the boundary between public decision preparation and advice or representation that requires source-specific review or an appropriate professional relationship.

Exact copy uniformity is not required. Route-specific language remains appropriate because route responsibility controls terminology. Confidence remains qualitative and thresholds remain descriptive.
