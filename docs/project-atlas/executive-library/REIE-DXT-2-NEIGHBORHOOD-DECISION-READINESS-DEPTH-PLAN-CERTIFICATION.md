## Governing Decision

Neighborhood Decision Readiness Depth should help the customer answer:

`Do I understand this place well enough to decide what to verify, compare, search, or inspect next?`

## Current Maturity

Neighborhood currently owns neutral place orientation, geography, local housing context, and transitions toward Search and Property evaluation.

The certified Market / City Market work clarified city-level readiness. The remaining material readiness gap is the place-level layer between city evidence and address-level Property evaluation.

## Responsibility Model

Neighborhood owns:

- neutral place orientation;
- geography;
- housing context;
- neighborhood-level evidence;
- verification prompts;
- transition to current inventory and address-level evaluation.

City Market owns city-level market evidence.

Search owns current property inventory.

Property owns address-level evaluation.

These responsibilities must remain distinct.

## Evidence Treatment

The future phase must remain route-local, evidence-focused, and place-orientation safe.

It should distinguish:

- evidence available on the Neighborhood route;
- evidence unavailable or requiring verification;
- directional place context;
- verified facts where supported;
- assumptions;
- freshness;
- unresolved unknowns.

It must not imply that REIE has completed personal verification for the customer.

## Confidence And Freshness

Confidence must remain qualitative, evidence-based, freshness-aware, and limitation-forward.

The future runtime must not introduce:

- numeric scores;
- letter grades;
- neighborhood rankings;
- best-neighborhood claims;
- investment confidence indicators;
- suitability conclusions;
- inferred preferences.

## Thresholds And Handoffs

Future certification should require clear thresholds for:

- when to return to City Market context;
- when to use Search for current inventory;
- when to open Property evaluation;
- when to prepare professional verification questions.

Direct Neighborhood entry must remain understandable without prior Market or City Market context.

Canonical URLs must remain clean and route-owned.

## Fair-Housing And Trust Boundaries

The future phase cannot introduce without separate authorization:

- neighborhood ranking;
- best-neighborhood claims;
- protected-class steering;
- demographic suitability;
- family-status assumptions;
- safety guarantees;
- unsupported crime conclusions;
- school-quality rankings or conclusions;
- personalized fit;
- investment recommendations;
- appreciation predictions;
- property or neighborhood scores;
- provider activation;
- API changes;
- map changes;
- hidden personalization;
- persistence;
- telemetry;
- shared readiness schemas;
- AI-generated professional conclusions.

Brokerage disclosure remains unchanged and under its existing external-review posture.
