# REIE Source Quality County Structured Evidence Conversion Contract MVV Certification

## Scope

This certification records a pure, repository-versioned conversion contract for explicitly supplied county/public-record structured evidence. The initial anchor is SRC-BOULDER-COUNTY-ASSESSOR.

## Bounded authority

The contract converts an exact source-bound, structured request into canonical SourceEvidenceLinkageRecord values for Source Quality normalization, control, and sparse assembly review. It requires explicit source confirmation, controlled certification metadata, reviewed date, finite field-sensitivity posture, and finite structured reference classes.

It does not create an Operational Manifest entry, activate a source, authorize retrieval, authorize scraping, grant legal use, grant customer display, or establish complete/current evidence.

## Evidence boundary

County correspondence, contact responses, documents, web prose, notes, and manual narrative are non-composable inputs. They require separately governed human review and structured conversion before they can be supplied to this contract. The protected county reconciliation artifact is outside this contract and was not read or used.

## Known-gap posture

The synthetic Assessor fixture proves architecture only. Its output retains UNKNOWN rights, technical-access, freshness, attribution, and provenance dimensions, and therefore canonical normalization/control remain insufficient/review-required.

## Firewalls

- Exact source-ID binding only; no aliases, matching, discovery, or enumeration.
- Public/government status does not imply unrestricted use, verified rights, completeness, currentness, technical access, attribution, or customer display.
- Structured rights/readiness evidence is not source activation.
- Field sensitivity is metadata only; no customer, owner, contact, address, parcel, or property-record value is accepted.
- Manifest eligibility requires a later separately authorized source-specific evidence package.

## Determinism and certification

The request, linkage output, and conversion fingerprint are deterministic from caller-supplied governed metadata. No clock or random identifier is generated. This certification does not create operational county evidence or modify Source Registry, Operational Manifest, application routes, or runtime retrieval.
