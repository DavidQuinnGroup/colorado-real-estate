## Evidence Treatment

Evidence available now is limited to existing Market-visible facts:

- city market paths;
- neighborhood paths;
- certified local guides;
- current market signals;
- existing Search continuations;
- Product 3 visual intelligence.

Evidence not yet available from Market includes property condition, inspection findings, taxes, HOA costs, insurance, seller motivation, buyer financing, title, contract risk, and personal suitability.

## Confidence Treatment

Confidence is descriptive and qualitative, not a score. It explains whether the broad market signal is organized enough to choose the next investigation surface. It does not recommend buying, selling, investing, timing the market, choosing a neighborhood, or relying on pricing certainty.

## Verification Treatment

The implementation keeps freshness and verification near the reliance point. Market direction remains directional until city, neighborhood, property, financing, inspection, records, or professional review verifies the specific question.

## Threshold Treatment

Market clarifies four customer thresholds:

- move to Search when the customer can name a market signal and wants to compare active inventory;
- open City Market when broad context is too general;
- inspect Neighborhood when the question is place organization, access, housing pattern, or verification conditions;
- open Property when public facts deserve address-level verification.

Search With Market Context remains preserved.

Product 3 remains preserved.
