## 11. Diminishing-Returns Assessment

Continued isolated Product Experience refinement is entering early diminishing returns.

Homepage Phase 2 and Advisory Phase 2 may each improve polish, but both primary problems are certified as resolved. Another buyer or seller feature may add surface area without a concrete unresolved decision. Another neighborhood or market enhancement risks returning to geographic work where LDI Wave 4 remains blocked and recent programs already addressed substantial geographic governance.

REIE should continue Product Experience work by standardizing the certified patterns rather than immediately refining another single surface. Standardization has higher enterprise leverage and lower production risk because it can create implementation guidance, acceptance criteria, primitive inventory, and validation expectations without touching runtime.
