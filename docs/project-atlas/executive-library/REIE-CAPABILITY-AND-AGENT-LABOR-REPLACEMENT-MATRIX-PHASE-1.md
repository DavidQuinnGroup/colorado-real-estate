## Executive Readout

REIE already replaces or reduces a large amount of repeatable agent research labor in five production-certified zones: property discovery/Search, visible property fact organization, source/evidence transparency, city/neighborhood/market orientation, and decision/preparation handoff. It standardizes many tasks that agents often repeat manually: explaining what a search result means, preparing questions before tours/offers, comparing visible property facts, explaining source limits, and routing customers toward the next evidence-based step.

REIE does not replace professional agency judgment. It does not automate negotiations, legal interpretation, appraisal, tax/lending advice, physical inspection, contract drafting, protected-class or demographic guidance, suitability conclusions, or property-specific due diligence that depends on external records not active in the repository.

The largest automation gaps are recurring newsletter production, full public-record/county-assessor retrieval, permit/zoning/HOA/flood/utility research, operational open-house work, live CRM workflow certainty, and scheduled alert/newsletter follow-through. Several of these have internal architecture or governance plans, but they are not all production-certified customer capabilities.

## Replacement / Reduction Summary

### Work REIE already substantially replaces

- Manual first-pass property search, filtering, map inspection, active inventory scanning, and basic property-fact extraction.
- Manual creation of many property, search, city, neighborhood, source, and decision-orientation explanations.
- Repetitive prompt generation for verification, professional handoff, offer preparation, buyer prep, seller prep, and source limitations.
- Factual side-by-side comparison of visible property facts, with explicit boundaries against ranking, scoring, suitability, valuation, and investment advice.
- Source-status explanation for customers, including blocked/not-authorized source states and county/source coverage posture.

### Work REIE reduces but does not replace

- Market brief preparation, seller listing preparation, buyer meeting prep, showing prep, offer-prep context, neighborhood/city education, and CRM follow-up preparation.
- Comparable/pricing research, because REIE can organize facts and context but cannot replace CMA methodology, offer strategy, appraisal, negotiation, or fiduciary advice.
- Public-record/government-source research, because REIE exposes source posture and verification prompts but does not actively retrieve assessor, parcel, permit, tax, zoning, flood, HOA, or utility data.

### Work still primarily human

- Negotiation, fiduciary advice, pricing strategy, legal/tax/lending/appraisal advice, contract execution, relationship management, physical property observation, inspection judgment, fair-housing compliance judgment, and final customer communication.

### Highest-value future automation gaps

1. Production-authorized public-record retrieval and source-rights expansion for assessor/parcel/tax/permit/zoning/HOA/flood/utility evidence.
2. Recurring market/newsletter/content generation with source freshness and human approval.
3. Operational saved-search alert cadence and changed-listing follow-up, if separately authorized.
4. CRM workflow hardening, including clear production mutation boundaries and agent-visible work queues.
5. Open-house/event preparation workflow that reuses property, market, comparison, and visitor-question intelligence without pretending to execute the physical event.
