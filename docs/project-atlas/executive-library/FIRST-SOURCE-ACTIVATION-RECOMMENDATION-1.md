# First Source Activation Recommendation(tm) 1.0

Status: `FIRST_SOURCE_ACTIVATION_RECOMMENDATION_CREATED`

Date: July 29, 2026

## Recommendation

Recommended first activation candidate:

`SRA-BOULDER-COUNTY-OPEN-DATA`

Decision posture:

`APPROVE_WITH_CONDITIONS`

## Rationale

Boulder County Open Data is the strongest first candidate because it offers:

- High customer value for housing-pattern and practical-context intelligence.
- Boulder County geographic coverage aligned to the priority city wave.
- Authoritative government source posture.
- Relatively clear open-data terms compared with public portals.
- Low privacy risk if limited to aggregate city-level derived observations.
- Better implementation reliability than portal scraping or document retrieval.

## Required Conditions Before Activation

1. Confirm exact dataset names.
2. Confirm dataset-specific license and exceptions.
3. Confirm storage, transformation, aggregation, and public-display rights.
4. Confirm attribution language.
5. Confirm export/API endpoint and rate limits.
6. Confirm retention and deletion obligations.
7. Exclude individual-owner intelligence from customer display.
8. Keep public output aggregate, attributed, and non-predictive.

## Activation Scope

The first activation should be narrow:

- One Boulder County open dataset.
- One or two cities.
- One domain such as housing-pattern or practical-context support.
- Internal persistence only after schema authorization.
- No customer-visible publication until editorial certification.

This recommendation is not legal advice and does not issue final legal conclusions.
