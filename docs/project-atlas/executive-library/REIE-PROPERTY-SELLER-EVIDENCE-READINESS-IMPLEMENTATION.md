## Foundation Scope Decision

It does not govern or infer property value, listing price, seller net proceeds, repair priority, property condition, legal ownership, title quality, insurability, permit compliance, disclosure sufficiency, marketability, sale timing, transaction probability, readiness score, lead priority, recommendation, or public evidence eligibility.

## Source Rights

Unknown and unresolved rights fail closed. Restricted evidence remains internal or blocked. Prohibited evidence remains blocked. Attribution requirements are preserved. Acquisition authority remains separate from display or derivative-use authority. Possession does not imply publication authority. No licensing conclusion is created and no provider is activated.

## Freshness And Versioning

The foundation models current, aging, stale, undated, superseded, historically valid, and unresolved postures through the certified Evidence Depth vocabulary. Undated evidence is not treated as current. Stale evidence creates review prompts only. Superseded evidence is preserved. No live refresh or external record fetch occurs.

## Conflict Handling

The contract preserves conflicting documents, differing dates, inconsistent property descriptions, competing municipal or HOA records, unresolved ownership or title questions, and conflicting inspection or specialist information.

It does not select an automatic winner, weight sources secretly, collapse conflict into certainty, or create condition or legal conclusions. It produces question-preparation and professional-review prompts only.
