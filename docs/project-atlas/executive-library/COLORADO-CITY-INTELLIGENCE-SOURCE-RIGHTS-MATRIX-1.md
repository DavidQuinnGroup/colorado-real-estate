## Governing Interpretation

Only repository-local city market data is candidate-ready in this wave. Every government, permit, recorder, planning, and imagery source remains blocked from durable storage or customer display until source-specific terms, attribution, field meaning, freshness, cost, credential, and reuse rights are approved.

No source in this matrix authorizes scraping, public GIS activation, customer-facing map overlays, customer-specific recommendations, school or safety rankings, demographic targeting, predictive claims, or investment guidance.
