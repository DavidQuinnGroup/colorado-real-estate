## Required Domains

The governed City Intelligence model still requires all seven domains before evidence-complete maturity:

- Housing patterns
- Neighborhood relationships
- Local access and practical context
- Balanced trade-offs
- Local imagery
- Market interpretation
- Verification questions

This wave does not complete all domains for any non-certified city.

## Blockers

- Assessor reuse rights are unresolved.
- Building-permit reuse and automation rights are unresolved.
- Municipal planning source reuse and effective-date handling are unresolved.
- Recorder document reuse, fees, and legal-interpretation boundaries are unresolved.
- City-specific imagery rights are unresolved.
- Durable persistence is not authorized.
- Provider execution is not authorized.

## Publication Gate

No city can advance to public evidence-backed publication from this matrix alone. Future publication requires source rights, complete evidence domains, resolved conflicts, imagery approval, Fair Housing review, balanced-language review, customer-usefulness review, freshness acceptance, and human editorial approval.
