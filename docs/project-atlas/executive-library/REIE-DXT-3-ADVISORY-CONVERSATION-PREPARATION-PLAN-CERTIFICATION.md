## Current Purpose Finding

Advisory remains the route/component-owned preparation experience before a focused professional conversation. It organizes evidence posture, open questions, professional limits, and next-step readiness without creating a submission, representation, hidden context, persistence, telemetry, CRM record, email, or scheduling behavior.

Contact remains the general conversation initiation path. Property Inquiry remains the specialized Property-specific inquiry path.

## Material Gap Finding

The certified plan identifies a bounded future opportunity to make Advisory more explicit about:

- the decision being prepared;
- evidence reviewed;
- evidence still needed;
- assumptions;
- unknowns;
- questions to verify;
- customer-readable conversation priority;
- appropriate professional pathway;
- what belongs on originating routes versus Advisory;
- what belongs on Contact.

The gap is preparation quality only. It is not a runtime defect and does not require immediate remediation.

## Evidence And Question Treatment

Future Advisory work may organize route-visible evidence categories only. It cannot infer or transfer hidden Property, Buyer, Seller, Search, Market, Neighborhood, financial, or customer-specific context.

It may identify categories of questions requiring source, Property, lender, market, legal, tax, insurance, title, inspection, appraisal, engineering, construction, or other professional review. It may not answer protected questions.

## Assumptions And Unknowns

The plan preserves explicit assumptions and unknowns as preparation concepts. Advisory may surface what is not yet known or not confirmed, but cannot convert those items into conclusions, recommendations, rankings, scores, suitability labels, professional advice, or customer-specific decisions.

## Conversation Priority Treatment

Conversation priority must remain static, visible, and customer-readable. It cannot become saved state, hidden context, CRM classification, lead scoring, telemetry, persistent preference, suitability label, recommendation, ranking, or professional conclusion.

## Pathway Treatment

- Advisory remains preparation before a focused professional conversation.
- Contact remains general conversation initiation.
- Property Inquiry remains the specialized Property-specific inquiry path.
- Originating routes retain ownership of route-specific evidence and questions.

## Visible-Context Plan

Potential future visible context may include:

- originating public route;
- public Property address or slug;
- public city or Neighborhood;
- visible route intent;
- static preparation category.

URL-context expansion, form prefill, persistence, telemetry, CRM integration, and hidden context remain separately gated and unauthorized.

## Privacy And Consent Plan

Future Advisory implementation must preserve:

- visible context only;
- no hidden transfer;
- no persistence;
- no telemetry;
- no customer profile;
- no prechecked consent;
- no implied marketing consent;
- no confidential information requested before an appropriate channel exists;
- existing specialized form consent and privacy behavior unchanged.

## Advice And Representation Boundaries

Advisory may organize questions but cannot provide legal advice, tax advice, lending advice, appraisal or valuation advice, pricing strategy, investment advice, suitability conclusions, fair-housing steering, representation claims, fiduciary claims, promised results, or AI impersonation of a licensed professional.

## Direct Entry And Anchor Preservation

The plan requires direct `/contact` entry to remain understandable without prior journey state. It also requires preservation of:

- `/contact#advisory-readiness`

- `/contact#contact-route-choice`

## Dominant-Action And Alternative-Continuation Model
