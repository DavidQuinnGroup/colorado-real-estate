## Boundary

`LICENSING RESOLUTION DOES NOT AUTHORIZE TECHNICAL CONNECTION OR PILOT EXECUTION`

Licensing and attribution resolution is an internal governance gate. It may identify exact official terms, source notices, limitations, attribution obligations, disclaimer obligations, use-right classifications, unresolved questions, legal-review gates, provider-confirmation gates, and conditions for a later technical-feasibility review. It does not authorize provider contact, accounts, credentials, terms acceptance, live services, provider data acquisition, adapter execution, persistence, retrieval, runtime activation, downstream integration, customer display, redistribution, relationships, hierarchy inference, Colorado runtime consumption, GOF Wave 5, or Sprint 9.

## Principles

- `GIS-LARG-P001 Exact Terms Principle`: licensing conclusions reference exact official terms, notices, metadata, or disclaimers applicable to the selected provider and dataset or service family.
- `GIS-LARG-P002 Public Access Is Not Permission Principle`: public availability does not establish operational-use, derivative-use, display, or redistribution rights.
- `GIS-LARG-P003 Dataset-Level Applicability Principle`: organization-wide statements do not automatically apply to every dataset or service.
- `GIS-LARG-P004 Terms Hierarchy Principle`: dataset-specific, portal-wide, agency-wide, state-wide, third-party, metadata, and disclaimer notices remain separate.
- `GIS-LARG-P005 Attribution Completeness Principle`: attribution preserves exact source identity, links, dates, notices, and unresolved wording.
- `GIS-LARG-P006 Disclaimer Preservation Principle`: official accuracy, completeness, hazard, and non-reliance limitations remain attached.
- `GIS-LARG-P007 Legal Review Boundary Principle`: Codex classifies uncertainty and review gates but does not provide legal approval.
- `GIS-LARG-P008 Rights Separation Principle`: access, research, operations, transient processing, retention, transformation, derivative use, display, redistribution, and commercial use are independent.
- `GIS-LARG-P009 Unknown Rights Fail Closed Principle`: unresolved rights are not implied permission.
- `GIS-LARG-P010 Minimum Retention Principle`: unresolved retention defaults to transient processing and governed audit metadata only.
- `GIS-LARG-P011 Attribution Before Output Principle`: no future derived or displayed output may proceed without required source attribution and disclaimers.
- `GIS-LARG-P012 Modification Transparency Principle`: modification or derivation notice requirements remain explicit.
- `GIS-LARG-P013 Third-Party Rights Principle`: third-party base maps, imagery, embedded services, and linked datasets do not inherit CGS permissions.
- `GIS-LARG-P014 Conflict Preservation Principle`: conflicting or unresolved official notices are preserved and escalated.
- `GIS-LARG-P015 Current Verification Principle`: current conclusions carry official sources and access dates.
- `GIS-LARG-P016 Resolution Is Not Execution Principle`: a resolved gate permits only consideration of a later non-acquiring technical design phase.
